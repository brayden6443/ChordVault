CHORD CHART BASICS

How to read

01

Strings

The six vertical lines represent the guitar strings, ordered low E through high e from left to right.

02

Frets

Horizontal lines divide the fretboard. The numbers on the right identify each fret position.

03

Finger dots

Purple dots show where to press. The black number inside each dot tells you which finger to use.

04

Open strings

A circle above a string means play that string open without pressing a fret.

05

Muted strings

An × above a string means do not play that string.

06

The nut

The thicker top line appears on open-position chords and represents the guitar’s nut.
